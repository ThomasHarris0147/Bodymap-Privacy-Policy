# Delete your account

**Bodymap — training log**
Published by Thomas Harris

You can delete your Bodymap account and everything logged in it at any time. There are two ways, and you do not need to contact us to use the first.

---

## In the app

1. Open **Profile** (the "You" tab).
2. Scroll to your account card.
3. Tap **Delete account**, then confirm.

It happens immediately. Nothing is held for a grace period and nothing is recoverable afterwards, so **export your data first if you want to keep it** — the dialog offers this, and Profile → Export produces a spreadsheet of every set you have logged plus a full backup file.

## Without the app

If you cannot reach the app — you have changed phone, uninstalled it, or lost access — email:

**thomasharris0147@gmail.com**

Send it from the address on the account, and say that you want the account deleted. We will confirm and complete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

---

## What is deleted

Everything that identifies you, and everything you logged:

- Your account and your email address
- Your display name and profile picture
- Every session, exercise, set, rep and weight you have recorded
- Every bodyweight weigh-in
- Your settings — units, split, goal, coverage target
- The copy of all of the above held on the device

## What is kept, and why

- **Records of a purchase.** If you ever subscribed to Bodymap Pro, the record of that purchase is retained as long as tax law requires — typically six to seven years. It is detached from your deleted account and no longer identifies you to us.

That is the only exception.

## How long it takes

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Deleted in the app | Immediately |
| Requested by email | Within 30 days, usually much sooner |
| Residual copies in database backups | Overwritten within 30 days |
| Records of a purchase | Retained 6–7 years, as tax law requires |

Our hosting provider takes rolling backups of the database, so a copy of deleted data can persist in those for a short period after the account itself is gone. Those backups are overwritten on a rolling basis and are never used to restore an account that has been deleted.

---

## Cancel your subscription first

**Deleting your account does not cancel a Bodymap Pro subscription.** Billing runs through Google Play, which knows nothing about your Bodymap account — so if you delete the account and leave the subscription running, you will keep being charged for it.

Cancel it in the Play Store first: **Play Store → your profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Bodymap**.

---

## If you only want to clear your data

You do not have to delete the account to get rid of what you logged. **Profile → Clear all data** erases every session and weigh-in, on the device and in your account, and leaves the account itself in place.
